Phishing

What is Phishing

Phishing is an online scam where hackers try to fool people into giving away personal information, like passwords, credit card numbers, or bank details. They usually pretend to be a company or someone you trust so that their message looks real.

How does it work?

  • You get an email, text, or message that looks real
  • The message tries to make you panic or act quickly
  • It asks you to click on a link that takes you to a fake website
  • If you type in your personal information, the hacker can steal it
  • They can then use your information to access your accounts or even steal your identity

How to stay safe?

  • Always check who sent the message
  • Don't click on links that look strange or unexpected
  • Turn on two-factor authentication
  • Use a password manager to help keep your passwords safe
  • Don't download attachments unless you're sure they're safe

From: IT Support Desk <support-portal-alert@microsoft-update-sec.com>

To keep your current password, or to set a new one, please verify your identity using the secure portal link below:

Click here to keep your current password

Note: Ignoring this notification will result in account suspension until your department manager manually resets the access.

Thank you,
Global IT Security Operations
