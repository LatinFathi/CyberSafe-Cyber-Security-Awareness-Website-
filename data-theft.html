Data Theft

Learn what data theft is, how it happens, and how to protect your personal information.

What Is Data Theft?

Data theft is when someone steals personal or private information without permission. This information could include passwords, bank details, phone numbers, or personal photos.

How Does Data Theft Happen?

  • Hackers find a way to get into your account or device.
  • They collect personal information like passwords or bank details.
  • They may use the information themselves or sell it to other criminals.
  • This can happen through phishing scams, malware, or weak passwords.

How to stay safe?

  • Use strong and different passwords for every account.
  • Turn on two-factor authentication.
  • Keep your software updated.
  • Avoid sharing sensitive information online.
  • Only enter personal information on websites you know and trust.
