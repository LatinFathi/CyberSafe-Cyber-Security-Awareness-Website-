Password Security

Learn how to create strong passwords and protect your accounts.

What is Password Security

Password security is about creating strong passwords and keeping them safe so other people can't get into your online accounts.

How does it work?

  • Every account has a password that only you should know.
  • Your password helps protect your personal information.
  • If someone guesses or steals your password, they can log into your account.
  • A strong password makes it much harder for hackers to break in.

How to stay safe?

  • Make your passwords at least 12 characters long.
  • Use a different password for every account.
  • Don't use personal information like your name or birthday
  • Include letters, numbers, and symbols in your passwords.
  • Must include capital and Small letters.
  • Don't include names of real places or people
