Online Privacy

Learn how to protect your personal information when you're online.

What is Online Privacy?

Online privacy is about keeping your personal information safe whenever you're using the internet.

How does it work?

  • Websites and apps collect information about what you do online.
  • When you make an account, you usually share things like your name or email address.
  • Hackers or scammers can use this information to target you or steal your identity.
  • Some of this information can become public if your privacy settings aren't secure.

How to stay safe?

  • Don't share too much home personal information online.
  • Check your privacy settings regularly.
  • Only accept friend requests from people you actually know.
  • Read the permissions an app asks for before downloading it.
  • Choose who can see your posts.
